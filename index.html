How to enable the keyboard

  • Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  • Tap Add New Keyboard → ReadyPaste
  • Tap ReadyPaste → turn on Allow Full Access
  • In any app, tap the globe key and choose ReadyPaste

Free & Pro

  • Free: up to 10 snippets, 1 folder, 3 pins
  • Pro: unlimited library, smart placeholders (name, date, time, clipboard), private iCloud sync
