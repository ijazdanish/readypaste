Enable the ReadyPaste keyboard

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app
  2. Go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  3. Tap Add New Keyboard… → choose ReadyPaste
  4. Tap ReadyPaste → turn on Allow Full Access
  5. Open Messages, Notes, or Mail → tap the globe key → select ReadyPaste

Snippets not inserting?

  • Confirm ReadyPaste is added as a keyboard and Full Access is on
  • Force-quit the host app (Messages/Notes) and try again
  • Open the ReadyPaste app once so your library can load
  • Restart your iPhone if the keyboard still does not appear

iCloud sync (Pro)

  • Sign in to iCloud on the device (Settings → [Your Name])
  • Unlock ReadyPaste Pro
  • In ReadyPaste → Settings → tap Sync Now
  • Use the same Apple ID on your other devices

Subscriptions & restore

  • Manage or cancel: iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • In ReadyPaste → Settings → Restore Purchases if Pro does not unlock after reinstall
  • Refunds are handled by Apple
